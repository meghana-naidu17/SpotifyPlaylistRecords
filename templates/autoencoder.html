Deep Non-Linear Audio Compression & Latent Mapping

Methodology

How Neural Autoencoders Work

1. The Encoder

Maps original 11 audio features into intermediate representations using non-linear activation functions, compressing dimensionality down to the bottleneck.

2. The Bottleneck (Z)

A low-dimensional information bottleneck that forces the network to retain only essential acoustic patterns while discarding noise and redundancy.

3. The Decoder

Symmetrically unpacks the compact latent vector back into reconstructed feature estimates, optimizing Mean Squared Error through backpropagation.

4. Non-Linear Power

Unlike PCA which is strictly linear, autoencoders use activation functions (ReLU, Tanh) to bend and model complex curved manifolds in the Spotify dataset.
