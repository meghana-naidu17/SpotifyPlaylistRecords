Method

How Automated Hierarchical Clustering Works

1. Preprocessed Data

Loads standard preprocessed features while excluding explicit target indicators.

2. Agglomeration & Dendrogram

Recursively merges the closest clusters according to the selected linkage metric.

3. Automatic K Selection

Computes Silhouette Scores for K = 2 through 8 to find the mathematically optimal number of clusters.

4. Direct Visualization

Renders dendrogram cutoff, silhouette curve, and 2D PCA cluster distribution directly without manual setup.
