Manifold Topology

Local vs. Global Structure Preservation

Linear methods like PCA struggle when data lies along non-linear curves. t-SNE maps high-dimensional probability distributions into 2D using Student-t distributions, isolating tight local song clusters. UMAP builds on Riemannian geometry and fuzzy simplicial sets, preserving both local groupings and the overarching global relationships between musical genres with superior speed.

Methodology

How Manifold Learning Works

1. t-SNE Probability

Converts Euclidean distances into conditional probabilities representing similarities, minimizing the Kullback-Leibler divergence.

2. UMAP Fuzzy Simplicial Sets

Assumes data lies on a Riemannian manifold and models the geometry via simplicial complexes, optimizing cross-entropy.

3. Perplexity & Neighbors

Perplexity (t-SNE) and N-Neighbors (UMAP) control the effective neighborhood size. Low values emphasize micro-clusters.

4. Scalability

UMAP leverages nearest neighbor descent algorithms, scaling cleanly to millions of points without retraining.
