Theory

Understanding Principal Component Analysis

1. Variance Maximization

PCA rotates the feature coordinates so that the first axis lies along the longest stretch of data variance, preserving the maximum amount of signal.

2. Orthogonality

Every subsequent component is strictly perpendicular (at a 90° angle) to all previous components, guaranteeing that they capture entirely zero redundant information.

3. Scree Plot Elbow

The "elbow" point on the scree plot indicates diminishing returns, helping you choose the minimal number of components necessary to preserve 80-90% variance.

4. Loading Coefficients

Loadings measure the correlation between original audio features and principal components, allowing human interpretation of what PC1 and PC2 musically represent.
