Method

How DBSCAN Works

1. EPS

EPS defines how far DBSCAN searches around each track to find neighboring observations.

2. Min Samples

Min Samples determines how many neighboring observations are required for a dense region.

3. Clusters

Dense areas are connected together to create clusters without specifying K beforehand.

4. Noise

Records that do not belong to a dense region are assigned the DBSCAN label -1.
